Our story

We were guessing. We stopped.

Griffin and Gavin started Wood's Waste out of Farmington, MN. Good operators, solid work — but pricing jobs on the fly meant inconsistent quotes, lost margin, and the occasional job we should've charged more for.

Customer calls, you're covered in sweat on someone's lawn, you throw out a number. Sometimes you nail it. Sometimes you leave $200 on the table. Sometimes you quote too high and lose the job.

So we built a calculator we could pull up on our phones in real time. Load size, special items, miles out, how long it'll take — plug it in and it spits out a number that actually covers your costs and hits your margin.

Other operators started asking about it. So now we're making it available. Two minutes to set up your rates. Works on any phone. No app install.

Features

Built for how the job actually works.

🎚️
Markup slider up to 500%
Slide it up when you really want the job or the customer is easy. Slide it down for a difficult site or a tight market. Your margin, your call — every single time.
⚙️
Your costs, locked in
Set your load rates, dump fees, labor rate, and drive cost once. Every quote after that is calculated off your real numbers — not industry averages.
📦
Special item surcharges
Mattresses, fridges, tires, pianos, hot tubs — toggle on whatever applies to the job and the fee gets added automatically.
🗺️
Drive distance factored in
Type in the miles and your per-mile rate kicks in automatically. Jobs 40 miles out should cost more than jobs down the street. Now they will.
📋
Job history log
Every quote you save gets logged with the price, load size, markup, and timestamp. Look back at past jobs and spot patterns.
📱
Works on any device
No app to install. Open it on your phone, tablet, or laptop. Works on iOS, Android, whatever. If it has a browser, it works.
