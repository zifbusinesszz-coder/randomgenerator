Our story

We were you a year ago.

Griffin and Gavin started Wood's Waste out of Farmington, MN. Good reputation, solid reviews — but too many jobs slipping away because we couldn't get back to people fast enough with a number.

Customers don't wait. They call three companies and go with whoever gives them a price first. We were usually third.

So we built an instant quote calculator for our own site. Customers pick a load size, flag any special items, and get a price range on the spot — before they ever pick up the phone.

The difference was immediate. More bookings, fewer no-answers, more jobs closed while we were still on a truck. We started getting calls from other operators asking how we did it. So here we are.
