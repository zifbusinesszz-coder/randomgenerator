FAQ

Junk removal in Farmington: common questions

How much does junk removal cost in Farmington?

Our minimum pickup in Farmington is $199 for a single item or small load. Bigger jobs are priced by how much truck space your load takes. As a guide: about $259 for a quarter load, $399 for a half, $539 for three-quarters and $699 for a full 15-yard load. Past a full load, each extra cubic yard is around $40. Farmington is in our home area: no travel fee on any job. Items with extra dump fees (couches, mattresses, Freon appliances, tires, TVs) add to the total, and you always get the full price before we start.

Do you offer same-day junk removal in Farmington?

Yes — same-day service is frequently available in Farmington since we're based right here. Call or text in the morning and we can often have a crew out the same day, typically within 2 hours.

What items do you take?

Almost everything: furniture, appliances, mattresses, electronics, construction debris, yard waste, garage contents and general clutter. We don’t take paint, chemicals or other hazardous waste.

Do I need to carry anything to the curb?

No. We do all the lifting and carrying — from any room, any floor, any part of your property. You just point at what needs to go.

What happens to my items after pickup?

We sort everything afterward. Usable furniture and household items are donated or resold — they get a second life instead of going straight to a landfill. Recyclables are sorted and handled properly.

Are you locally owned?

Yes. Wood's Waste is a small, owner-operated business run by Griffin and Gavin out of Farmington, MN. When you call, you're talking to the people who'll actually show up and do the work.

Do you do estate, basement and move-out cleanouts in Farmington?

Yes. We handle whole-house estate cleanouts, basement cleanouts and rental move-outs in Farmington, priced by volume with the full price up front.
