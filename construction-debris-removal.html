Construction debris removal

Cleanup That Keeps Your Project Moving

Renovation projects generate a lot of debris fast — and construction waste can't go out with regular garbage pickup. Drywall, lumber scraps, tile, flooring, and concrete all need to be properly hauled and disposed of. Renting a dumpster works for some jobs, but often you just need someone to come haul it away.

Wood's Waste handles construction debris cleanup for homeowners and contractors. Whether it's post-remodel cleanout, a demo job, or ongoing cleanup during a project, we load up the truck and take it to a licensed disposal facility. We can work around your crew and your schedule.

Same-day service available across the Twin Cities metro. Call or text and we'll get back to you within 2 hours.

FAQ

Construction debris removal: common questions

What types of construction debris do you remove?

We remove all types of renovation and construction waste — drywall, lumber, flooring, tile, concrete, roofing shingles, insulation, cabinets, countertops, windows, doors, and general demo debris. Whether it's a small bathroom remodel or a full gut renovation, we'll haul everything out. Call or text (507) 298-1179 if you have a specific material you're unsure about.

How much does construction debris removal cost in the Minneapolis metro?

Our minimum is $199, which covers a small load. Larger debris piles are priced by how much truck space the load takes up. Heavy materials like concrete and tile may carry a weight surcharge depending on volume. We give you a clear quote before we start — no surprises at the end of the job. As a guide: about $259 for a quarter load, $399 for a half, $539 for three-quarters and $699 for a full 15-yard load. Past a full load, each extra cubic yard is around $40. Items with extra dump fees (couches, mattresses, Freon appliances, tires, TVs) add to the total, and you always get the full price before we start.

Do I need to bag or sort the debris before you arrive?

No. You don't need to bag, sort, or stage anything. We load everything ourselves — loose drywall, scattered lumber, piles of tile, whatever the job left behind. Just point us to the debris and we'll take care of the rest. The only thing that helps is making sure we have reasonable access to the area.

Can you remove debris during an active renovation project?

Yes — mid-project cleanouts are one of the most common jobs we do. Keeping a jobsite clean makes the work go faster and safer. We can schedule regular pickups throughout a larger project or come out for a one-time haul when debris has piled up. Same-day service is frequently available throughout the south metro.

Do you remove concrete, brick, or heavy masonry?

Yes, we remove concrete, brick, block, and other heavy masonry debris. These materials are heavier per volume than typical construction waste, so pricing reflects that. We'll give you an accurate quote based on the amount before we start. Call or text (507) 298-1179 to describe what you have and get a quick estimate.

Is construction debris recycled or just landfilled?

We sort and recycle what we can — metal, clean wood, concrete, and other recyclable materials are separated and processed appropriately rather than dumped wholesale. Minnesota has strict disposal requirements for certain construction materials and we follow them on every job. Not everything can be recycled, but we make the effort where it's possible.

Do you serve contractors and remodelers, or just homeowners?

Both. We work with homeowners doing DIY projects, contractors managing active job sites, and remodelers who need reliable debris hauling throughout a project. If you're a contractor looking for a consistent hauling partner in the Minneapolis south metro, give us a call and we can work out a setup that fits your schedule.
