Cleanup That Keeps Your Project Moving

Renovation projects generate a lot of debris fast — and construction waste can't go out with regular garbage pickup. Drywall, lumber scraps, tile, flooring, and concrete all need to be properly hauled and disposed of. Renting a dumpster works for some jobs, but often you just need someone to come haul it away.

Wood's Waste handles construction debris cleanup for homeowners and contractors. Whether it's post-remodel cleanout, a demo job, or ongoing cleanup during a project, we load up the truck and take it to a licensed disposal facility. We can work around your crew and your schedule.

Same-day service available across the Twin Cities metro. Call or text and we'll get back to you within 2 hours.
