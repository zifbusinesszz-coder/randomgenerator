We were guessing. We stopped.

Griffin & Gavin Wood · Wood's Waste, Farmington MN

FAQ

Common questions

Is this just a pricing calculator or a full business tool?

It's both, and that's the point. Starter gives you the quote calculator, AI photo quoting, special item fees, and basic job history. Pro adds a full customer CRM, job scheduling with a calendar, route planning, before & after photo documentation, job timers, and profit analytics. Team adds crew management on top of all that.

How does scheduling work?

After you quote a job, add the customer's name, phone, address, and a date/time. The job shows up on your calendar and your upcoming jobs list. On the day of the job, tap it to start the timer, get directions, and log the outcome when you're done.

How do junk removal operators price jobs?

Most operators price by load size — minimum, quarter, half, or full truck — then add special item fees for things like mattresses, fridges, and tires, plus a markup over their actual cost. Wood's Waste Pro automates this using your exact dump fees, drive rate, and labor cost so every quote is based on real numbers.

Is there an app to download?

No App Store needed. Wood's Waste Pro works in any phone browser. On iPhone, add it to your home screen for one-tap access that works like a real app. On Android, tap "Add to home screen" from Chrome. It works on desktop and tablet too.

How does the AI photo quoting work?

Upload a photo of the pile. The AI estimates load size, identifies special items like mattresses and refrigerators, and generates a quote using your saved rates. It takes about 10 seconds. You can edit the result before saving.

Can I track what I actually charged vs. what I quoted?

Yes. After each job, log what you actually charged and how long it took. The Insights dashboard breaks this down by load size — so you can see where you're consistently undercharging, which jobs are most profitable, and how your revenue trends over time.

How does the Team plan work?

The owner invites crew members by email. Members join with their own login and see the jobs assigned to them. The owner assigns jobs from the dashboard — members get an email notification and their job list updates when they log in. 3-seat plan supports 1 owner + 2 members. 10-seat plan supports 1 owner + up to 9 members.
