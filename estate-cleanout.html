Estate cleanouts

A Whole House, Handled With Care

Clearing out a loved one’s home is a lot to take on, both physically and emotionally. Wood’s Waste handles the heavy part so your family can focus on what matters. We remove furniture, appliances, clothing, kitchenware, and everything in the basement and garage, room by room.

Before anything leaves, we work around the items you want to keep. Usable furniture and household goods get donated, and recyclables are sorted out. Only what’s left goes to the dump.

We work with families, executors and realtors across the south metro. Nobody needs to be there while we work if you’d rather not be, and we’ll send photos when the house is empty.

FAQ

Estate cleanouts: common questions

How much does an estate cleanout cost?

Estate cleanouts are priced by volume. Most homes take one or more full loads. We give you a firm price up front from photos or a quick walkthrough, so you know the total before we start. As a guide: about $329 for a quarter load, $479 for a half, $629 for three-quarters and $779 for a full 15-yard load. Items with extra dump fees (mattresses, Freon appliances, tires, TVs) add to the total, and you always get the full price before we start.

Do you donate items from the estate?

Yes. Usable furniture and household items get donated, and recyclables are sorted out before anything goes to the dump.

Does someone need to be at the house?

No. Many families and realtors give us a lockbox code or meet us to unlock. We send photos when the job is done.

Can you work with the realtor or executor?

Yes. We coordinate directly with whoever is handling the property, and we can schedule around showings and closing dates.

How long does an estate cleanout take?

Most houses are done in a day. Very full homes can take longer, and we’ll tell you up front.

What can’t you take?

We don’t take paint, chemicals or other hazardous waste. Everything else in a typical home, we haul.
