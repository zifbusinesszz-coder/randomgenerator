FAQ

Good questions

How much does junk removal cost?

Our minimum pickup is $199, which covers a single item or a small load. Larger jobs are priced by how much truck space your load takes. A full garage or estate cleanout is quoted by volume before we start. No hidden fees.

Do you offer same-day junk removal?

Yes. Same-day service is often available across our area. Text or call in the morning and we can usually have a crew out the same day.

What items do you take?

Almost everything: furniture, appliances, mattresses, electronics, construction debris, yard waste, garage contents and general clutter. Paint, chemicals and propane tanks need special disposal, so there’s an extra charge for those.

Do I need to carry anything to the curb?

No. We do all the lifting and carrying from any room, any floor, any part of your property. Just point at what needs to go.

What happens to my stuff after pickup?

We sort everything. Usable furniture and household items get donated, and recyclables are recycled. Only what’s left goes to the dump.

Are you locally owned?

Yes. Wood’s Waste is owner-operated by Griffin and Gavin out of Farmington, MN. You’ll talk to the people who actually do the work.
