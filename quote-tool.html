Our story

We were guessing. We stopped.

Griffin and Gavin started Wood's Waste out of Farmington, MN. Good operators, solid work — but pricing jobs on the fly meant inconsistent quotes, lost margin, and the occasional job we should've charged more for.

Customer calls, you're covered in sweat on someone's lawn, you throw out a number. Sometimes you nail it. Sometimes you leave $200 on the table.

So we built a calculator we could pull up on our phones in real time. Then we added AI — point your phone at the pile, and it estimates load size, spots the mattress and the fridge, and quotes using your own rates.

Other operators started asking about it. So now we're making it available.
