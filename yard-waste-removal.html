Yard Waste Removal — Common Questions

We remove all types of yard waste — brush, branches, logs, stumps, leaves, grass clippings, garden debris, old mulch, dirt, rocks, landscaping materials, and general outdoor clutter. Whether it's leftover from a storm, a landscaping project, or years of accumulation, we'll haul it out. Call or text (507) 298-1179 if you have something specific you're unsure about.

Our minimum is $150, which covers a small load. Larger jobs are priced by how much truck space the load takes up. Heavy materials like dirt, rocks, and concrete carry a weight surcharge depending on volume. We give you a clear quote before we start — no hidden fees, no numbers that change at the end. Call or text for a fast estimate.

No bagging required. We load loose brush, branches, and debris directly — you don't need to prep anything ahead of time. If things are already bagged that's fine too, but it's not something we require. Just show us what needs to go and we'll handle the loading and hauling.

Yes — stumps, large logs, and heavy wood debris are all things we handle. We don't do stump grinding, but if a stump has already been ground down or pulled out we'll haul away the material. Large logs from tree removal or storm damage are also no problem. Just let us know what you have when you call so we can bring the right setup.

Yes — dirt, gravel, rock, pavers, retaining wall blocks, and old landscaping materials are all things we remove. These are heavy per volume so pricing reflects that, but we'll give you an accurate quote based on what you have before we start. Call or text (507) 298-1179 to describe the job and get a quick estimate.

Organic yard waste like brush, branches, leaves, and grass clippings is taken to composting and green waste facilities rather than a landfill where possible. Rocks and concrete are recycled or taken to appropriate disposal sites. We follow Minnesota disposal regulations on every job and make an effort to keep material out of the landfill whenever there's a responsible alternative.
