We Do the Heavy Lifting — All of It

Getting rid of a couch, a king-size mattress, or an entire bedroom set is harder than it looks. Most donation centers won't pick up. The dump requires a truck and a plan. That's where Wood's Waste comes in — we show up, carry it out, and make it disappear.

We handle furniture removal from any room in the house — basements, upstairs bedrooms, tight hallways, you name it. We're careful with doorways and floors, and we leave the space broom-clean when we're done.

Whenever possible, we donate usable furniture to local organizations so it stays out of the landfill. Call or text and we'll respond within 2 hours.
